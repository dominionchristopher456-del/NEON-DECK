Features

Built to play together

Online and Wi-Fi rooms

Share a 4-letter room code and play from anywhere, or host a game on your home Wi-Fi with no internet needed.

Friends and requests

Add friends by name or ID, even when they are offline. They get a request and accept it when they next open the game.

Levels and leaderboard

Every game earns XP. Climb from Rookie to Legend, keep a win streak and see your place among the best players.

Voice chat and computer players

Talk to your table in online rooms. Short a player? The host adds a computer player in one tap.

Clear on every turn

Cards you can play glow, the discard pile shows the chosen colour, and a badge pops up on whoever gets hit by a Skip, +2 or +4.

Spin to start

A spinning bottle picks who goes first, after a 3, 2, 1 countdown before every match.

Game modes

Nine ways to play

Every mode changes the rules. The host picks one in the lobby before each match.

CLASSIC

The normal game. First player with no cards left wins.

SURVIVAL

Only power cards. Reach 12 cards and you are out. Last player standing wins.

STACK ATTACK

Stack +2 and +4 on top of each other. Can't stack? Draw the whole pile.

SWAP 7-0

Play a 7 to swap hands with the next player. Play a 0 and everyone passes their hand.

CHAOS

Surprises after some cards: the direction flips, a free card, or shuffled hands.

POWER-UPS

Everyone gets one secret power to use once: Shield, Steal, Freeze or Extra Turn.

LIGHTNING

12 seconds per turn. Run out of time and you draw a card and lose your turn.

BOOMERANG

Play a +2 or +4 and it flies back at you. Think before you strike.

MEGA HAND

Everyone starts with 14 cards. A long, wild race to empty your hand.

Support

Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that normal?

Yes, for new apps that are not yet widely downloaded. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer is the one from this page.

Do I need internet to play?

Not for single player or Wi-Fi games on the same network. Online rooms, friends and the leaderboard need an internet connection.

How many players can join a game?

Up to 4 players in one room, and any seat can be filled by a computer player.

If I change my name, do I lose my progress?

No. Open your profile and press Change Name. Your ID, XP, friends and leaderboard place stay the same.

Does it cost anything?

No. Neon Deck is free to download and play.
