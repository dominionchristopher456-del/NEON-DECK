Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

What this policy covers

This policy explains how the Neon Deck website and game handle information. Neon Deck is a free card game for Windows.

The game

  • Your player name, friend ID, profile picture, friend list, XP and wins are saved on your own device.
  • When you play online, the game sends your player name, friend ID, profile picture, XP and wins to our online server so friends, rooms and the leaderboard can work. Your name, ID and score are shown to other players. Choose a name that does not reveal your real name.
  • Voice chat in online rooms is passed between players in the same room and is not recorded or stored by us.
  • We do not ask for your address, phone number or payment details inside the game.
  • If you sign in with Google, the game receives your name and email address from Google to give you one account on all your devices.

The website

This website does not ask you to create an account. If ads are turned on, advertising partners such as Google may use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Children

Neon Deck is a card game that anyone can enjoy, but we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond a chosen player name and a random friend ID. Children should ask a parent or guardian before using online features, voice chat or sign-in. A parent or guardian who wants information removed can contact us at the address below.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information shown on the leaderboard and in rooms is visible to other players.

Deleting your information

Information saved on your device is removed when you uninstall the game and delete its saved files. To ask for your name and score to be removed from our online server, contact us with your friend ID.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: use the contact details on the Neon Deck website.
